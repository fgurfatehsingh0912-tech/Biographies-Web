Sarah Jenkins

Sarah Frances Jenkins (born 1998) is an award-winning British contemporary classical composer and clarinettist. She has quickly risen to prominence as one of the UK’s most compelling young musical voices, known for creating striking, atmospheric sound worlds heavily inspired by the natural world.Early Life and BreakthroughRoots: Born in 1998, Jenkins grew up with a deep passion for music, influenced by her parents, mentors, and early participation in youth orchestras.The Turning Point: Her career reached a major breakthrough in 2017 when she was named the winner of the prestigious BBC Young Composer competition. Following this landmark win, she was appointed as a BBC Proms Inspire Ambassador.Major Commissions and Career HighlightsSince her breakthrough, Jenkins has been regularly commissioned by some of the UK's most elite orchestras and chamber ensembles:BBC Concert Orchestra: In 2018, her piece And the Sun Stood Still was premiered live on BBC Radio 3 at the Queen Elizabeth Hall under the baton of conductor Bramwell Tovey.The BBC Proms: Her acclaimed work Music and Meditation was performed at the Royal Albert Hall as part of The Dream Prom 2022.Presteigne Festival: She was appointed as the festival's first-ever "Emerge Composer" from 2022 to 2024. Her solo violin suite, Tincture of the Skies, premiered here and was later released on violinist Fenella Humphreys' album PRISM.Recent Works: In 2025, her piano piece I am out with Lanterns, Looking for Myself was premiered on BBC Radio 3 to celebrate International Women’s Day.
