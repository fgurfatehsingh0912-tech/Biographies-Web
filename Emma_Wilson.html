Emma Wilson

Emma Wilson (born 7 April 1999) is an elite British windsurfer and two-time Olympic Bronze Medalist. Widely regarded as one of the greatest windsurfers of her generation, she currently reigns as the 2025 iQFOiL World Champion. Representing the British Sailing Team, she has successfully dominated across two distinct eras of windsurfing equipment—transitioning seamlessly from traditional boards to high-performance hydrofoils.Early Life and Olympic PedigreeRoots: Born in Nottingham, England, Wilson grew up in a household deeply rooted in elite sports. She spent her formative years training on the waters of Dorset.Family Influence: Her mother, Penny Way, is a windsurfing icon who competed as a two-time Olympian for Great Britain at the 1992 and 1996 Games. Rather than feeling pressured by her mother's legacy, Wilson credits her family as her primary competitive edge.Youth Prodigy: She proved to be a phenom from an early age, winning her first world title at just 12 years old at the U15 Techno 293 World Championship. She went on to achieve a stunning hat-trick of gold medals at the Youth Sailing World Championships in 2014, 2016, and 2017.Senior Breakthrough and Tokyo 2020 OlympicsTransitioning to senior international fleets, Wilson rapidly established herself as a consistent threat in the RS:X windsurf class.Tokyo Olympic Debut: At the delayed 2020 Tokyo Olympic Games, Wilson secured Team GB's first women's windsurfing medal since 2008 by winning the bronze.Near Miss: Proving how tight the competition was, she finished the regatta just two points shy of the gold medal. The agonizingly close finish became her primary motivator to chase future global titles.Adapting to iQFOiL and Paris 2024Following the Tokyo Olympics, the international windsurfing landscape underwent a massive change as the Olympic committee replaced the RS:X class with the ultra-fast iQFOiL class (hydrofoil boards reaching speeds of up to 65 km/h).Mastering the Foil: Wilson adjusted rapidly to the dangerous, fast-paced discipline despite severe crashes during early training. She took silver at the Olympic test event in Marseille.Paris 2024 Olympics: At the Paris Games, Wilson put on a dominant display throughout the opening series off the coast of Marseille. After a dramatic final medal race, she secured her second consecutive Olympic Bronze Medal.World Champion and Current DominationRather than stepping back after the Olympics, Wilson used the momentum to enter the most dominant phase of her career.World Championship Gold: At the 2025 iQFOiL World Championships in Aarhus, Denmark, she overcame an early crash in the final race to pull away from Israel's Tomar Steinberg and claim Senior World Championship Gold.European Success: She added an iQFOiL European Championship silver medal to her tally in Sicily later that year.2026 Season Launch: Wilson began her 2026 season flawlessly by securing overall victory at the iQFOiL International Games in Lanzarote, defeating reigning Olympic champion Marta Maggetti.Home Title Defense: She is currently preparing to defend her global crown on her home training waters at the 2026 iQFOiL World Championships in Weymouth & Portland, UK.
