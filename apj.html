Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was an eminent Indian aerospace scientist and statesman who served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007. Widely revered as the "Missile Man of India," he played a crucial role in developing India's civilian space program and military missile capabilities. His humble beginnings, profound scientific contributions, and deeply inspiring presidency earned him the title of the "People's President."Early Life and EducationBirth: Born on October 15, 1931, in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.Family: Raised in a humble family; his father was a poor boat owner.Early Work: Distributed newspapers as a boy to support his family's finances.Graduation: Earned a degree in Science from St. Joseph's College, Trichy, in 1954.Engineering: Specialized in Aeronautical Engineering from the Madras Institute of Technology (MIT) in 1957.Scientific CareerISRO Milestone: Served as the Project Director for India's first indigenous Satellite Launch Vehicle (SLV-III).Satellite Launch: Successfully injected the Rohini satellite into near-Earth orbit in July 1980.Missile Development: Led the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP) at DRDO, developing key strategic missiles like Agni and Prithvi.Nuclear Capability: Played a pivotal role as the technological leader during the Pokhran-II nuclear tests in May 1998.Presidency (2002–2007)Election: Elected almost unanimously as the 11th President of India on July 25, 2002.Public Connection: Transformed the presidency by opening the Rashtrapati Bhavan to ordinary citizens, particularly youth and students.Vision: Focused extensively on a roadmap to transform India into a developed nation.Later Life and LegacyTeaching: Returned to academia as a professor at Anna University and other premier institutions.Inspiration: Dedicated his final years to interacting with high school students to ignite young minds.Passing: Suffered a fatal cardiac arrest on July 27, 2015, while delivering a lecture at IIM Shillong.World Students' Day: His birthday, October 15, is widely observed as World Students' Day in his honor.Major Awards and BooksBharat Ratna (1997): Received India's highest civilian honor.Other Honors: Awarded the Padma Bhushan (1981) and Padma Vibhushan (1990).Famous Memoirs: Authored the bestselling autobiography "Wings of Fire".Visionary Books: Penned inspiring works including "Ignited Minds" and "India 2020".
